# Infinite Water?

You may have been wondering if perhaps there was a way to insert water into the boiler automatically? There is! That's exactly what the water pump does. Surround it horizontally with water blocks, and it will start producing water for 1RF/t.
It will automatically transfer water to the machine on its output side.

The Bronze Water Pump will produce 1/8th of a bucket of water for every adjacent water source every 5 seconds, for a max output of 1 bucket / 5 seconds.
