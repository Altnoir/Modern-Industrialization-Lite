# Better Diesel

## Stainless Steel Diesel Generator

Keep in mind you can use a Stainless Steel Diesel Generator for compact power generation. As you might expect, it produces up to 1024 RF/t. Remember that you can find the amount of energy stored in every fuel with REI.
