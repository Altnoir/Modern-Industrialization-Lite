# Advanced Machines

## Aluminum

<ItemImage id="milite:aluminum_ingot" />

Make the first aluminum alloy in an electric blast furnace. Its machine hull unlocks the professional machines described here.

The Centrifuge splits items by spinning them very fast. You can use it to make carbon dust and unlock better recipes for steel and silicon.

<Recipe id="milite:processing_table/aluminum_alloy_centrifuge" />

The Chemical Reactor can make items and fluids react.

<Recipe id="milite:processing_table/aluminum_alloy_chemical_reactor" />

The Distillery will turn various fluids into their lighter parts. That will be very useful for Oil Processing.

<Recipe id="milite:processing_table/aluminum_alloy_distillery" />

The Electrolyzer can split materials into their different parts using strong electric currents. You can electrolyze bauxite dust to get aluminum dust, which you can then smelt into aluminum ingots in a regular Furnace or Blast Furnace.

<Recipe id="milite:processing_table/aluminum_alloy_electrolyzer" />

The Configurable Tank is the fluid equivalent of the Configurable Chest.

It has 9 configurable slots that can each hold 16 buckets, and auto-eject functionality! Useful for automation with many different fluids!

<Recipe id="milite:processing_table/configurable_tank" />
